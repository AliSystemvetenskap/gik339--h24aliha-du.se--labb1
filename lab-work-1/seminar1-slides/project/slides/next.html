StarkFit · Group 15 · 13

BILAL: To sum up: StarkFit grew by buying other gym chains, and now has systems that don’t work together. Our project, One Member, puts one member register at the centre and rolls it out one gym group at a time. We will keep using StarkFit for the rest of the course. In the coming labs we will build on today’s models: a data model for members, memberships, visits, bookings and payments; the system architecture, showing how the register connects to doors, booking and payments; the web information architecture of the app and website; and the platform architecture, where the systems run and how they handle the January rush.

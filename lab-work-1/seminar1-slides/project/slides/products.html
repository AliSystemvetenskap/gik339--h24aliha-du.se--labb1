StarkFit · Group 15 · 3

ALI: StarkFit’s products fall into three categories. First, memberships: monthly with direct debit, a yearly card, a student membership and day passes. Second, training: the gym floor, which is open from five in the morning to eleven at night, group classes like spinning, yoga and HIIT, and personal training. Third, some extras, like training plans in the app and drinks and supplements sold at reception. The membership is the core product. Everything else depends on knowing who the member is and what they have paid for.

BILAL: Our first architecture model is a business process model in BPMN. It shows the process “becoming a member”. We have three lanes: the member, the StarkFit system and the payment provider. The member chooses a membership and fills in their details. Then the system makes a decision, shown by the diamond: has this person been registered before? This is important for us, because duplicate members are one of StarkFit’s biggest problems. If yes, the system uses the existing record. If no, it creates a new one. Then the payment provider sets up direct debit or card payment. Finally the system activates access, and the member can enter any gym. That’s the end event, the thick circle.

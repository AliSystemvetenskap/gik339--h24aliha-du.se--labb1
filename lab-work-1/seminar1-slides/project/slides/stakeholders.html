Key stakeholders

Who has an interest in StarkFit

Members

Train at any gym, easy booking, correct bills

Owners

Growth, profit and a clear picture of the business

Managers

Gym and regional managers who need reliable numbers

Gym staff

Reception and trainers who help members every day

Head office

IT, finance and customer service who run the systems

System suppliers

Door, booking and app suppliers; some contracts end

Payment partners

Bank, direct debit and card provider

Authorities

GDPR and IMY: personal data must be protected

Stakeholder: anyone who affects or is affected by the organisation (Freeman, 1984) · Group 15 · 6

ALI: These are StarkFit’s key stakeholders. We use Freeman’s definition: anyone who affects the company or is affected by it. The most important are the members, because they pay for everything, and the owners, who want growth and profit. Inside the company we have managers, gym staff and the head office teams like IT and finance. Outside the company we have the system suppliers, where some contracts will end with our project, payment partners like the bank and card provider, and the authorities, since StarkFit handles a lot of personal data and must follow GDPR.

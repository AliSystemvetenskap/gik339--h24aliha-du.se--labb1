Process: steps that together create value for a customer (Dumas et al., 2018) · Group 15 · 9

BILAL: These are StarkFit’s main business processes. We describe them as the member’s journey. One: join, where you pick a membership, sign up and get access. Two: visit, where you scan your tag or app and the door checks that your membership is active. Three: book a class, and the trainer sees who is coming. Four: pay every month, and if a payment fails the member gets a reminder. Five: leave, where access stops and personal data is deleted on time. Every one of these steps needs the member register, which is why the project focuses on it. We also have one change process for the project itself: moving a gym to the new system. We copy the data, remove duplicates, connect the doors and booking, test, and only then turn the old system off.

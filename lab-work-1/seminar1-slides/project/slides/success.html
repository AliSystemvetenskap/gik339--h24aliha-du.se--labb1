StarkFit · Group 15 · 12

BILAL: So what must StarkFit get right for this to succeed? We see five success factors. One: every member can actually use every gym, because that is the promise to the customer. Two: the doors and booking must never stop, even while we switch systems. A gym where members can’t get in is a big failure. Three: clean data, one person, one record. Four: staff who know and trust the new system, so training the staff is part of the project. And five: personal data that is safe and deleted on time, to follow GDPR.

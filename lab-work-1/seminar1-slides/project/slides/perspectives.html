StarkFit · Group 15 · 7

ALI: Here are four perspectives on the company. Customers think: I pay for all gyms, so let me use all of them, with one app and correct bills. Employees, like reception staff, want to find a member in one system instead of three, so they can spend time helping people. Managers want correct numbers on members, visits and income, so they can compare gyms and make decisions. And the sponsors, which are the owners who pay for the project, want lower system costs, since today they pay for many systems, and a platform that makes it easy to buy and add more gyms in the future.

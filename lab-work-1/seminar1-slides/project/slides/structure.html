Organisational structure

Head office plus four regions

Management: CEO · CFO · Head of Operations

Head office (shared services)

  • IT – 4 people (leads the project)
  • Finance – 3
  • Customer service – 5
  • Marketing – 3
  • HR – 2

Operations

4 regional managers

25 gym managers (one per gym)

Reception, trainers, personal trainers

A “divisionalized form” (Mintzberg, 1979) · Group 15 · 8

BILAL: Thanks Ali. This is how StarkFit is organised. At the top is the management team: the CEO, the CFO and the Head of Operations. Under them there are two parts. On the left, the head office, with shared services for all gyms: IT with four people, finance, customer service, marketing and HR. The IT team leads our One Member project. On the right, operations. Four regional managers each follow up a group of gyms, every gym has its own gym manager, and under them are the receptionists, trainers and personal trainers. Mintzberg calls this a divisionalized form: each region and gym runs its daily work, while head office decides strategy and runs shared systems.

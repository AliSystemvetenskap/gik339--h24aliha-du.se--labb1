ALI: Our project is called One Member. The idea is simple: every member should exist only once, and be able to use every gym. At the centre is one shared member register. The doors, class booking, payments, the app and the reports all connect to it. We don’t switch everything at once. We move one gym group at a time, so the business never stops. We set six goals, and each one can be measured. All 25 gyms open to every member by the end of 2027. Zero duplicate members. Doors and booking can’t be down for more than one hour when a gym switches. The system must handle three times the normal sign-ups in January. Reports should be created automatically. And personal data of people who leave must be deleted on time, according to GDPR.

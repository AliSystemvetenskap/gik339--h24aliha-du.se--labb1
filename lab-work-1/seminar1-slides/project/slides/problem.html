StarkFit · Group 15 · 4

ALI: So what is the problem? Each chain StarkFit bought came with its own systems, and they were never joined together. Today StarkFit runs three membership systems, two door systems, two booking apps and three different ways of handling payments. This causes real problems. A member who joined in Borlänge can’t get into a former GymNorr gym in Gävle, even though StarkFit says you can train everywhere. The same person can exist two or three times, so nobody knows the real number of members. Head office builds reports by hand. Old member data is not always deleted, which is a GDPR risk. And every January, when lots of people join, the systems slow down. This can’t be fixed by just buying a new app, because the gyms must stay open and the doors must keep working while we change things.

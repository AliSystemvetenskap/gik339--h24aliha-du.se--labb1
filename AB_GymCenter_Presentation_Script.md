## SLIDE 4 – The problem today

**On the slide:** Members · Staff · Business

**🎤 ALI:**
> Today, booking is messy and manual.
> It causes problems for three groups.
>
> **Members** can't easily see if a class has free spots.
> Booking is slow, and they don't get reminders.
>
> **Staff** spend a lot of time on the phone and at the desk.
> They change bookings by hand and answer the same questions again and again.
>
> **The business** has little insight.
> People book and don't show up, so spots are wasted.
> And our member data is weak.
>
> Because of all this, many members get tired and leave after a few months.

---

## SLIDE 5 – The project

**On the slide:** Member app → Booking system

**🎤 ALI:**
> So our project is simple:
> **one member app, one booking flow, and one place for all the data.**
>
> In the **member app**, members can find classes, book or cancel,
> join a waitlist, and get reminders.
>
> Behind the app is the **booking system**.
> It handles schedules, how many places each class has, and who attended.
>
> Some things are not part of the project.
> For example, opening a second gym, buying new machines, or making online videos.

---

## SLIDE 6 – SMART goals

**On the slide:** S · M · A · R · T

**🎤 ALI:**
> Our goals are SMART.
>
> **Specific:** we launch one app for class booking and attendance.
> **Measurable:** 80 percent of class bookings are made by the members themselves.
> **Achievable:** we start with a basic version that covers the most important needs.
> **Relevant:** less admin work for staff, and easier access for members.
> **Time-bound:** we test it after 4 months, and check the results after 6 months.
>
> We also want fewer members to leave, and fuller classes.
> Today classes are about 60 percent full. We want 80 percent.

## SLIDE 10 – Four perspectives

**On the slide:** Member · Process · Information · Technology

**🎤 BILAL:**
> We look at the project from four sides.
> This way it is not only about technology.
>
> **Member:** booking should be easy, fast and clear.

---

## SLIDE 11 – Enterprise architecture

**On the slide:** Experience · Services · Process · Data · Platform

**🎤 BILAL:**
> Here is our enterprise architecture. It has five layers.
>
> At the top is **experience** – the member app and the staff portal.
> Then **services** – booking, waitlist and notifications.
> Then **process** – scheduling, reserving and checking in.
> Then **data** – members, classes and attendance.
> At the bottom is the **platform** – login, connections to other systems, and monitoring.
>
> So when a member taps "book" in the app,
> it goes down through all the layers and gets saved.

---

## SLIDE 12 – Book and attend a class

**On the slide:** 1 Browse → 2 Book → 3 Remind → 4 Check in → 5 Record

**🎤 BILAL:**
> This is our main process, step by step.
>
> **Step 1, Browse:** the member looks at the schedule and free spots.
> **Step 2, Book:** they book a spot, or join the waitlist if it's full.
> **Step 3, Remind:** they get a reminder before the class.
> **Step 4, Check in:** they check in when they arrive at the gym.
> **Step 5, Record:** the system saves who attended.
>
> If a member doesn't show up many times,
> the reception can see it and contact them.
> And if someone cancels, the next person on the waitlist gets the spot.
